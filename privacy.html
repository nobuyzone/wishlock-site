Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Wishlock (“Wishlock,” “we,” “us,” “our”) is a wishlist and gift-coordination app and website (wishlockapp.com) based in Kelowna, British Columbia, Canada. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Wishlock iOS app, our website and any other place that links to this policy (together, the “Services”). We are responsible for the personal information we process. If you don’t agree with this policy, please don’t use the Services.

The short version
  • We collect what you need to make lists, invite people and gift each other.
  • The person a list belongs to can never see who claimed or bought what.
  • We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t use it to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • Wish-trend insights are off unless you turn them on. If you do, only anonymous, combined statistics are shared with brands.
  • We use your general area (like your city), never your precise location.
  • You can delete your account and data in the app at any time, and contact us about your rights at hello@wishlockapp.com.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number if you choose to add one, and a sign-in identifier (for example from Sign in with Apple). If you use Sign in with Apple’s “Hide My Email,” we receive a relay address instead of your real one.
  • Your lists: list names, occasions and dates, wishes (names, notes, links, prices, tags, photos and videos), gift preferences, sizes and favourites, and your “All set on” notes.
  • People you invite: the names, email addresses or phone numbers you enter or pick to invite gifters or Secret Santa participants. Please only invite people who would expect to hear from you.
  • Gifting activity: gifts you claim, split or mark as bought, gifter chat messages, Secret Santa draws and anonymous questions, and giving drive pledges.
  • Creator content: if you post Creator Picks, your posts, product links and profile details.
  • Preferences: your notification and privacy settings, and the city or region you choose for local recommendations.
  • Feedback and reports: messages you send us and content you report.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device type and model, operating system and app version, language, and a push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Log and usage information: IP address, dates and times you use the Services, which features are used, and crash and error reports, so we can keep the Services secure, fix problems and improve them.
  • General location: your approximate area (such as your city or province), worked out from the city you choose or your IP address, to show local gift ideas in Get Inspired and local experiences. We do not collect your precise (GPS) location and we do not track your location in the background.

Device permissions

The app asks before using these features, and you can change your choice anytime in your iPhone’s Settings:

  • Contacts: so you can pick people to invite. Only the contacts you choose are sent to us. We never upload your whole address book or use it for anything else.
  • Photos and camera: so you can add photos or videos to wishes and posts. Only the items you pick are uploaded.
  • Calendar: if you choose to add an occasion date to your calendar.
  • Notifications: to send the alerts you’ve allowed.

Sensitive information

We don’t ask for sensitive information such as health, religion, ethnicity or sexual orientation. Because you can wish for anything, some wishes might hint at these things. We treat wishes as private to you and the people you share them with, and we leave sensitive product categories (such as health and medical items) out of Wish-trend insights.

Information from others

Someone may invite you by entering your name, email or phone number, or add you to a Secret Santa. We use those details only to deliver that invite. If you don’t join, we delete them after the invite expires. Apple tells us your subscription status when you subscribe to Wishlock Plus.

Current beta: in the early beta, your lists are saved only on your own device and are not sent to our servers.

2. How we use your information

  • To run Wishlock: create and manage your account, show your lists to the people you share them with, deliver invites, and keep claims hidden from the list owner.
  • To let people communicate: gifter chat, Secret Santa messages and anonymous questions.
  • To send notifications you’ve allowed, such as claims by other gifters, sale alerts on lists you’re gifting, and Secret Santa messages.
  • To send service messages, such as account and security notices and changes to our terms or this policy.
  • To show local recommendations in Get Inspired, based on your general area.
  • To keep Wishlock safe: filter inappropriate words and images, review reports, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To provide Wishlock Plus and apply creator codes.
  • To understand and improve the Services, using usage trends and error reports.
  • To create Wish-trend insights, only if you turn them on (see section 4).
  • To meet legal obligations and protect anyone’s safety in an emergency.

We don’t use your information to build advertising profiles, and we don’t make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects using automated means alone.

3. Consent and legal bases

Canada. We rely on your consent. Some of it is implied by using the Services (for example, storing the lists you create), and some is express, meaning you actively say yes (for example, Wish-trend insights, notifications and device permissions). You can withdraw consent at any time, though some features may stop working. In limited cases the law allows us to collect, use or disclose information without consent, such as to investigate fraud or a breach of an agreement, to comply with a court order or law, or in an emergency that threatens someone’s life, health or security.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. We process personal information only when we have a legal basis: to perform our contract with you (providing the Services), with your consent (which you can withdraw), for our legitimate interests (such as keeping the Services secure and improving them, where those interests aren’t outweighed by your rights), to comply with the law, or to protect someone’s vital interests.

4. Wish-trend insights (optional)

When you launch a list, we ask whether you’d like to turn on Wish-trend insights. If you do, we may include your wishes in anonymous, combined statistics that we share with or sell to brands and retailers, such as “linen throws are a top Christmas wish in BC this year.” These statistics use information like product category, brand, price range, occasion, general region (city or province) and whether something was gifted.

They never include your name, contact details, precise location, contacts, your list, or who gifted what. We only report on groups large enough that no one can be singled out, we don’t allow buyers to try to re-identify anyone, and we leave out sensitive categories. This setting is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time in Me › Privacy. Turning it off stops your future wishes from being included.

If you choose to tell us why you let an item go in “Thinking about it,” that reason is used in the same anonymous way.

5. Affiliate links, perks and partners

Some links in Wishlock are affiliate links. When you buy through them, we or a creator may earn a commission. When you tap one, you leave Wishlock, and the store (and its affiliate network) may know you came from Wishlock and use its own cookies under its own privacy policy. We may add our own affiliate tag to plain store links, but we never replace a creator’s own affiliate link.

Gifter Perks and Get Inspired content come from partner brands and local businesses. Partners do not receive your personal information.

Partners who advertise local experiences receive combined, anonymous reports, for example how many people added their experience to a wishlist. Counts under 10 are shown as “fewer than 10,” and reports never identify anyone.

6. Who we share information with

  • The people you choose. Your list is visible to the gifters you invite. Gifters can see each other’s claims and chat; the list owner cannot. Creator Picks you post are public.
  • Service providers who help us run Wishlock and may only use your information to do that work for us, under contracts that require them to protect it: our database and hosting provider (Supabase), Apple (sign-in, notifications and payments), app build and crash-reporting tools, and email delivery and moderation services. Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages.
  • Legal reasons, if required by law, a court order or a lawful request from authorities, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Wishlock, our users or others.
  • Business transfers, if Wishlock is sold, merged, financed or reorganized. The new owner would have to continue protecting your information as described here, and we would tell you before your information became subject to a different policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising (targeted ads based on your activity across other companies’ apps and websites). Anonymous, combined statistics that can’t identify you are not personal information.

7. Our website, cookies and tracking

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, tracking pixels or retargeting tools. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google to download them (see Google’s privacy policy). Our host, GitHub, keeps standard server logs for security.

The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask your permission first through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Do Not Track: because we don’t track you across sites, there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. If we ever add tracking, we will honour these signals as an opt-out where the law requires.

8. Payments

Wishlock Plus is purchased through Apple. Apple handles your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never see your card number or billing address. We receive confirmation of your subscription status.

9. Where your information is stored

We store your information in Canada with our hosting provider. Some of our service providers, including Apple and email and crash-reporting services, may process information in the United States or other countries. Information processed outside Canada is subject to the laws of that country, and may be accessible to its courts and authorities. Where we transfer personal information from the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we use recognized safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your lists, wishes and chat messages within 30 days, and remove them from backups within a further 60 days. We may keep limited records longer where we need to for legal, tax, safety or fraud-prevention reasons, and then only for as long as necessary. Invites that aren’t accepted are deleted when they expire. Anonymous, combined statistics cannot be linked back to you and may be kept.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and server rules that keep claims hidden from list owners. No system is perfectly secure, so please use Sign in with Apple or a strong password and keep your device secure. If a breach of your personal information creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the relevant privacy regulators as the law requires.

12. Your choices and rights

  • In the app: update your details, turn Wish-trend insights, Get Inspired and notifications on or off, and delete your account in Me › Privacy › Delete account.
  • On your iPhone: change contacts, photos, calendar and notification permissions in Settings.
  • By email: ask to access or get a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, withdraw consent, or ask how it has been used and who it has been shared with. Email hello@wishlockapp.com.

We respond within 30 days (we’ll tell you if we need more time and why). We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we use the information you give us only for that purpose. You can ask someone to make a request for you, but we may ask for proof they’re authorized. We won’t treat you differently for using your rights.

13. Additional information by region

Canada

Canadian privacy laws, including PIPEDA, British Columbia’s PIPA and Quebec’s Law 25, give you the rights in section 12. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, or Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information.

United States

Depending on your state (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon and others), you may have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use and disclose it; to access, correct, delete or get a portable copy of it; and to opt out of its sale, of targeted advertising and of profiling with significant effects. We don’t sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or profile you in that way, and we have not done so in the past 12 months. To use your rights, email us. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision; if your appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general.

In the past 12 months, and on an ongoing basis, the categories of personal information we collect are: identifiers (name, email, phone, account and device IDs, IP address); customer records (name and contact details); commercial information (your Wishlock Plus subscription status); internet or app activity (feature usage and error logs); general location (city or province); and content you create (wishes, photos, videos and messages). We collect them from you, your device, and people who invite you, for the purposes in section 2, and disclose them only to the parties in section 6.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

You have the right to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict or object to its processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office, or the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner. We’d appreciate the chance to sort it out first, so please contact us.

14. Children and teens

You must be at least 13 to use Wishlock, and if you’re under the age of majority where you live, you need a parent or guardian’s permission. Wishlock is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. In Quebec, we need a parent or guardian’s consent for users under 14. Wish-trend insights are not available to users under 16. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Wishlock grows or the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make important changes, such as using your information for a new purpose or sharing it with a new type of third party, we’ll let you know in the app before they take effect and ask for your consent where required.
