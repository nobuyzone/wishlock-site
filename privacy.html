Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Wishlock (“Wishlock,” “we,” “us”) is a wishlist and gift-coordination app operated by Aliyah Holroyd in Kelowna, British Columbia, Canada. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • We collect what you need to make lists, invite people and gift each other.
  • The person a list belongs to can never see who claimed or bought what.
  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • Wish-trend insights are off unless you turn them on. If you do, only anonymous, combined statistics are shared with brands.
  • You can delete your account and data in the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and a sign-in identifier (for example from Sign in with Apple). If you use Sign in with Apple’s “Hide My Email,” we receive a relay address instead of your real one.
  • Your lists: list names, occasions and dates, wishes (names, notes, links, prices, tags, photos and videos), gift preferences, sizes and favourites, and your “All set on” notes.
  • People you invite: the names, email addresses or phone numbers you enter to invite gifters or Secret Santa participants. Please only invite people who would expect to hear from you.
  • Gifting activity: gifts you claim, split or mark as bought, gifter chat messages, Secret Santa draws and anonymous questions, and giving drive pledges.
  • Creator content: if you post Creator Picks, your posts, product links and profile details.
  • Feedback and reports: messages you send us and content you report.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device type, app version, and a push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Basic usage information: which features are used and error reports, so we can fix problems and improve the app.

We do not collect your precise location. For local experiences, we use the city you choose.

Current beta: in the early beta, your lists are saved only on your own device and are not sent to our servers.

2. How we use your information

  • To run Wishlock: create your account, show your lists to the people you share them with, deliver invites, and keep claims hidden from the list owner.
  • To send notifications you’ve allowed, such as claims by other gifters, sale alerts on lists you’re gifting, and Secret Santa messages.
  • To keep Wishlock safe: filter inappropriate words and images, review reports, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To provide Wishlock Plus and apply creator codes.
  • To answer your questions and improve the app.

3. Wish-trend insights (optional)

If you turn on Wish-trend insights, we may include your wishes in anonymous, combined statistics that we share with brands and retailers, such as “linen throws are a top Christmas wish in BC this year.” These statistics use information like category, brand, price range, occasion and whether something was gifted. They never include your name, contact details, your list, or who gifted what. This setting is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time in Me › Privacy.

If you choose to tell us why you let an item go in “Thinking about it,” that reason is shared the same anonymous way.

4. Affiliate links, perks and partners

  • Some links in Wishlock are affiliate links. When you buy through them, we or a creator may earn a commission. The store may know you arrived from Wishlock. We may add our own affiliate tag to plain store links, but we never replace a creator’s own affiliate link.
  • Gifter Perks and Get Inspired content come from partner brands and local businesses. Partners do not receive your personal information.
  • Partners who advertise local experiences receive combined, anonymous reports, for example how many people added their experience to a wishlist. Counts under 10 are shown as “fewer than 10,” and reports never identify anyone.

5. Who we share information with

  • The people you choose. Your list is visible to the gifters you invite. Gifters can see each other’s claims and chat; the list owner cannot.
  • Service providers who help us run Wishlock, under contracts that require them to protect your information: our database and hosting provider (Supabase), Apple (sign-in, notifications and payments), and email delivery and moderation services.
  • Legal reasons, if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.
  • Business transfers, if Wishlock is sold or merged, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Payments

Wishlock Plus is purchased through Apple. Apple handles your payment details; we never see your card information. We receive confirmation of your subscription status.

7. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored with our hosting provider in [region to confirm when the backend is set up]. It may be processed outside your province or country by our service providers, subject to the protections described here.

8. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your lists, wishes and chat messages within 30 days, except where we need to keep limited records for legal or safety reasons. Anonymous, combined statistics cannot be linked back to you and may be kept.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in Me › Privacy › Delete account.
  • Turn off Wish-trend insights, Get Inspired and notifications at any time.
  • Ask to access or correct your information, or withdraw consent, by emailing us. Canadian privacy laws, including PIPEDA, BC’s PIPA and Quebec’s Law 25, give you these rights.

10. Children

Wishlock is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and server rules that keep claims hidden from list owners. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password or Sign in with Apple.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we’ll let you know in the app before they take effect.
